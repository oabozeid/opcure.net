How it is designed to work

Age-related cataract develops largely through oxidative damage to the lens's long-lived proteins. Some of the lens's antioxidant defenses depend on zinc.

  1. Topical zinc

    Each drop supplies zinc to the front of the eye.

  2. Lens epithelium

    The single cell layer under the lens capsule, where most of the lens's protective machinery sits.

  3. Antioxidant defenses

    Zinc-dependent defenses in these cells help neutralize oxidants.

  4. Intended outcome

    Less oxidative damage to the lens's long-lived proteins, so clouding progresses more slowly.

What sets it apart

Opcure is designed to support the lens's own defenses with an ingredient already used in the eye, rather than a new molecule or an oral supplement.
