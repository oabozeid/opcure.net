The Problem

Cataracts Remain the
#1 Cause of Global Blindness

Cataracts are the leading cause of blindness worldwide, affecting 94 million people with vision impairment. The only approved treatment is surgery — an intervention that is invasive, infrastructure-dependent, and inaccessible in much of the world.

The U.S. FDA has not approved any non-surgical pharmacological therapy to prevent, delay, or reverse cataracts.

Sources: Nature Eye 2024; WHO/IAPB Vision Atlas 2024; FDA Orange Book (April 2026).

Our Solution

A Patented Topical Therapy
with a Dual Mechanism of Action

Zinc Sulfate 0.30% — therapeutic active, within the Japanese Pharmacopoeia range of 0.27–0.33%. Chlorpheniramine Maleate 0.06% — antihistamine, improves tolerability. Carboxymethyl Cellulose 0.50% — lubricant and viscosity agent.

Dosage: 2 drops, four times daily (Q6H). Self-administered and room-temperature stable.

🧬

Restores SOD1 Activity

Zinc re-metallates Cu/Zn-SOD (SOD1), reactivating lens antioxidant defenses that neutralize the reactive oxygen species driving cataract formation.

Mechanism 1
⚗️

Saturates Zinc Reservoirs

Exogenous Zn²⁺ re-saturates metallothionein to the fully loaded Zn7 form, restoring the zinc buffer and breaking the oxidative-stress feedback loop.

Mechanism 2
🛡️

Evidence Base

Three Converging Lines of Evidence

Pre-clinical models, observational data, and a granted patent portfolio built on established actives.

Pre-clinical

  • 67% reduction in cataract severity in a selenite-induced rabbit model (Abdul-Hussein 2014, p<0.01)
  • 83% decrease in lipid peroxidation; 140% increase in glutathione
  • Attenuated diabetic cataract progression in a rat model (Barman 2018)

Observational

  • ~90% positive response across 230 volunteers
  • 91.4% estimated effectiveness in 175 cataract-specific cases (corrected count and conservative recount; 93.6% of 171 as printed in the granted patent)
  • No serious adverse events; only transient mild stinging
  • Caveat: uncontrolled data — the RCT is designed to provide rigorous evidence

IP & Safety

  • US Patent 11,433,096 granted (Sep 2022); China granted; PCT pending in the EU, Canada and India
  • All actives within international pharmacopoeia limits
  • Established safety profile; no new-ingredient CMC risk

The 100-eye Phase III RCT — six-month treatment with efficacy follow-up to Month 12, double-blind and placebo-controlled, at an Egypt site — is designed to convert observational signal into rigorous controlled evidence.
